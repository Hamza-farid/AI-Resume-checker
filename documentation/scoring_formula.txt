╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║          🧮 SCORING FORMULA - HOW THE FINAL SCORE WORKS   ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

   🐍 Python does ALL of this math. The AI only gives scores.
   Will be built in: core/scoring.py (Milestone 5)


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 1️⃣  THE BIG PICTURE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ⭐ FINAL (out of 100)  =  🎯 Role Match (30)  +  📊 Sections (70)

   ┌───────────────┬───────┬───────────────────────────────┐
   │ Part          │ Marks │ What it checks                │
   ├───────────────┼───────┼───────────────────────────────┤
   │ 🎯 Role Match │  30   │ Does the candidate meet HR's  │
   │               │       │ requirements? (checklist)     │
   │ 📊 Sections   │  70   │ How GOOD is each part of the  │
   │               │       │ resume? (7 sections)          │
   ├───────────────┼───────┼───────────────────────────────┤
   │ ⭐ Total      │ 100   │                               │
   └───────────────┴───────┴───────────────────────────────┘


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 2️⃣  THE WORDS WE USE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ┌────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ Word       │ Meaning                                 │
   ├────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────┤
   │ Priority   │ HR picks: Low / Medium / High           │
   │ Value      │ Low = 5 · Medium = 8 · High = 10        │
   │            │ = the MAX marks of that section         │
   │ AI score   │ The AI's number, out of 10              │
   │ Marks got  │ Value × AI score ÷ 10                   │
   └────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────┘


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 3️⃣  THE FORMULA (3 steps)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   STEP 1 → For each section:
            Marks got = Value × AI score ÷ 10

   STEP 2 → Add up:
            Total marks got   (all 7 sections)
            Total max marks   (all 7 values)

   STEP 3 → Scale to 70:
            📊 Sections = Total marks got ÷ Total max marks × 70
            🎯 Role Match = AI role score × 3
            ⭐ FINAL = Sections + Role Match       (out of 100)
            ⭐ Out of 10 = FINAL ÷ 10


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 4️⃣  FULL EXAMPLE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ┌────────────────────┬──────────┬───────┬──────────┬──────────────┬───────────┐
   │ Section            │ Priority │ Value │ AI score │ Calculation  │ Marks got │
   ├────────────────────┼──────────┼───────┼──────────┼──────────────┼───────────┤
   │ 💼 Experience      │ High     │  10   │  8 / 10  │ 10 × 8 ÷ 10  │    8.0    │
   │ 🛠️ Skills          │ High     │  10   │  7 / 10  │ 10 × 7 ÷ 10  │    7.0    │
   │ 🚀 Projects        │ Medium   │   8   │  9 / 10  │  8 × 9 ÷ 10  │    7.2    │
   │ 🎓 Education       │ Low      │   5   │  6 / 10  │  5 × 6 ÷ 10  │    3.0    │
   │ 📄 Formatting      │ Medium   │   8   │  7 / 10  │  8 × 7 ÷ 10  │    5.6    │
   │ 🧾 Summary         │ Low      │   5   │  5 / 10  │  5 × 5 ÷ 10  │    2.5    │
   │ 🏅 Certifications  │ Low      │   5   │  6 / 10  │  5 × 6 ÷ 10  │    3.0    │
   ├────────────────────┼──────────┼───────┼──────────┼──────────────┼───────────┤
   │ TOTAL              │          │  51   │          │              │   36.3    │
   └────────────────────┴──────────┴───────┴──────────┴──────────────┴───────────┘

   📊 Sections   = 36.3 ÷ 51 × 70  =  49.8 / 70
   🎯 Role Match = 7 × 3           =  21.0 / 30
                                      ──────────
   ⭐ FINAL                         =  70.8 / 100   →   7.1 / 10


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 5️⃣  3 QUICK CASES (same candidate, different priorities)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   AI scores: Exp 8 · Skills 7 · Projects 9 · Edu 6 ·
              Format 7 · Summary 5 · Certs 6 · Role Match 7

   ┌────────────────────────┬───────┬──────────┬──────────┬───────┐
   │ Case                   │ Max   │ Marks got│ Sections │ FINAL │
   │                        │ marks │          │  (/70)   │ (/10) │
   ├────────────────────────┼───────┼──────────┼──────────┼───────┤
   │ A. All High            │  70   │  48.0    │  48.0    │  6.9  │
   │ B. All Low             │  35   │  24.0    │  48.0    │  6.9  │
   │ C. Mixed (example ↑)   │  51   │  36.3    │  49.8    │  7.1  │
   └────────────────────────┴───────┴──────────┴──────────┴───────┘

   ✅ A = B → if all priorities are equal, High or Low
      doesn't matter
   ✅ All High → max = 70 → "marks got ÷ 70 × 70" = marks got
   ✅ C is higher → the candidate is strong where HR said "High"


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 6️⃣  SPECIAL CASE: N/A SECTION
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   If a section is N/A (for example no certifications, and the
   job doesn't need them):
     → it is LEFT OUT of both "marks got" AND "max marks"
     → the other sections share the 70 marks
     → the candidate is NOT punished for a missing section

   Example: Certifications N/A in the table above
     Marks got = 36.3 − 3.0 = 33.3
     Max marks = 51 − 5    = 46
     Sections  = 33.3 ÷ 46 × 70 = 50.7 / 70

 8️⃣  WHO DOES WHAT
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   🤖 AI      → 7 section scores (1-10) + Role Match score (1-10)
   🐍 Python  → everything in this file
   👤 HR      → picks Low / Medium / High, makes the final
                hiring decision

   Settings in config.py:
     ROLE_MATCH_MARKS = 30
     SECTIONS_MARKS   = 70
     PRIORITY_VALUES  = low 5 · medium 8 · high 10
