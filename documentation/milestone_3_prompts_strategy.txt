 1️⃣  THE BIG IDEA
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   HR controls WHAT matters for the job.
   The rubric controls what a score MEANS (7/10 = always the same).

 5️⃣  THE SCORING FORMULA  (Python does this, not the AI)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ⭐ FINAL (100) = 🎯 Role Match (30)  +  📊 Sections (70)

   🎯 ROLE MATCH (30 marks)
      AI gives 1-10  →  marks = score × 3
      Example: 7/10 → 21 / 30

   📊 SECTIONS (70 marks)
      Low = 5   ·   Medium = 8   ·   High = 10
      Each section's max marks = its value.

      Step 1: marks got = value × AI score ÷ 10  (each section)
      Step 2: add them up, and add up the max marks
      Step 3: sections marks = (marks got ÷ max marks) × 70

   ── EXAMPLE ─────────────────────────────────────────────
   Section         Priority  Max   AI score   Got
   Experience      High      10    8/10       8.0
   Skills          High      10    7/10       7.0
   Projects        Medium     8    9/10       7.2
   Education       Low        5    6/10       3.0
   Formatting      Medium     8    7/10       5.6
   Summary         Low        5    5/10       2.5
   Certifications  Low        5    6/10       3.0
                            ────             ────
                             51              36.3

   Sections   = 36.3 ÷ 51 × 70   = 49.8 / 70
   Role Match = 7 × 3            = 21.0 / 30
                                   ──────────
   ⭐ FINAL                       = 70.8 / 100  →  7.1 / 10
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   ✅ All High → max = 70 → just "marks got / 70"
   ✅ Works for ANY mix of Low / Medium / High
   📄 Full tables, 3 cases, N/A rule → see  scoring_formula.txt


══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 6️⃣  IMPORTANT RULES WE DECIDED
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   ❌ AI never decides percentages or the final score
      (it would change every run → unfair, and AI is bad at math)

   ❌ Priority never changes a score
      High section → AI gives MORE feedback + interview questions
      but 7/10 means the same in every section

   ✅ No double counting
      Role Match = CHECKLIST  ("does SQL exist? yes / no")
      Sections   = QUALITY    ("how well is SQL proven?")

   ✅ Missing section → N/A (not zero) → left out of the math
      (so no "Off" option is needed)

   ✅ Scores are whole numbers only (1, 2 ... 10)

   ✅ JSON order: evidence → pros → cons → summary → score
      AI looks at the PROOF first, THEN picks the number
      → more accurate + more consistent scores

 8️⃣  WHAT THE AI RETURNS (JSON)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   candidate_snapshot   → headline, latest title, years, seniority
   role_match           → must-have ✅🟡❌ list, nice-to-have,
                          experience fit, summary, score
   sections (×7)        → applicable, evidence, pros, cons,
                          summary, confidence, score
   pros / cons          → top strengths + weaknesses overall
   red_flags            → cheating, date problems, overlaps
   hr_instruction_flags → biased HR notes NOT applied
   interview_questions  → question + reason + section
   analytics            → facts for charts (see below)
   overall_summary      → 2-3 lines for a manager
   recommendation       → strong / good / possible / weak match

   ➕ Python adds later: "final" → sections marks, role marks,
      total /100, score /10, label

 🆕  PROMPT v1.2 - RED FLAGS (prompts/base/flags.md)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

   The AI finds the flags itself (no Python checks), using
   the rules in flags.md. Research-based: surveys say most
   lies are about skills, experience, degree, job title;
   hidden AI instructions are growing (≥1% of resumes).

   🔴 HIGH (clear contradiction or trick)
      manipulation_attempt   hidden "rate me 10/10" text
      experience_mismatch    "10+ years" but dates = 4 years
      impossible_dates       end before start, future dates
      impossible_skill_claim more years than the tool exists
   🟡 MEDIUM (ask about it)
      overlapping_roles      2 full-time jobs at once
      title_inflation        "Head of" 1 year after graduating
      education_irregularity master's without bachelor's
      unrealistic_numbers    intern "+900% revenue"
      copied_job_description resume = job post copy
      keyword_stuffing       huge skill list, no proof
   ⚪ VERIFY (background check, not an accusation)
      needs_verification     unknown certificate / award
      other

   EVERY FLAG = type · 📌 exact quote · what doesn't add up ·
                💬 interview question · severity

   NEVER FLAGGED: gaps, short jobs, career change, grammar,
                  "looks AI-written", protected traits
   Flags never change a score by themselves; false claims
   just get no credit.
   The AI also gets today's date (for future dates + tool age).
