## Section: `certifications` (Certifications & Achievements)
Do certifications, awards, publications, or competitions add real value for **this** job?

- 9-10: Respected, job-relevant certifications, or notable achievements (awards, publications, competition wins).
- 7-8: Relevant certifications or achievements.
- 5-6: Some are relevant, others are generic (for example short intro courses).
- 3-4: Mostly unrelated.
- 1-2: Only trivial items.
- Not applicable: none listed. Missing certifications are normal and must not count against the candidate unless the job requires a specific one.
