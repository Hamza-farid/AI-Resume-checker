## Section: `experience` (Work Experience)
How relevant, deep, and proven is the work history for **this** job? Internships, freelance, and part-time work count, weighted by how relevant and substantial they are. (Whether the required *number of years* is met belongs to Role Match; here, judge the quality of the work.)

- 9-10: Closely related work with measurable impact (numbers, scale, ownership) and clear growth over time.
- 7-8: Relevant duties with some measurable results.
- 5-6: Related work, but duties are listed without results, or relevance is partial.
- 3-4: Mostly unrelated work, or very little of it.
- 1-2: No meaningful work content for a job that needs it.
- Not applicable: an entry-level or fresher job where no work experience is listed.

Look for: action verbs with results, responsibility and scope, promotions, tools used on the job. Check that the dates make sense.
