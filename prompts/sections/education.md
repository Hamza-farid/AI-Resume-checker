## Section: `education` (Education)
Does the education fit what this job needs? Judge the **field and level**, never how famous the institution is.

- 9-10: Degree or training directly relevant to the job, plus extras such as a thesis, relevant coursework, or honors.
- 7-8: Relevant degree or training.
- 5-6: A degree in a partly related field.
- 3-4: An unrelated field, and the job asks for a related one.
- 1-2: The job clearly requires a degree and none is shown.
- Not applicable: no education listed, and the job does not require it.
