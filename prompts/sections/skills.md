## Section: `skills` (Skills Depth)
How strong and **proven** are the candidate's skills for this job? (Whether each required skill is present at all is the Role Match checklist; here, judge depth and proof.)

- 9-10: Key skills are clearly proven through real use in jobs or projects, with advanced or specialized depth.
- 7-8: Most relevant skills are backed by some real use.
- 5-6: Relevant skills are mostly listed, with little proof of use.
- 3-4: Few relevant skills, or mostly unrelated ones.
- 1-2: Almost no relevant skills.

A long keyword list with no proof of use is not a strength.
