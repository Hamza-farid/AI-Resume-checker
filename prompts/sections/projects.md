## Section: `projects` (Projects & Portfolio)
Do personal, academic, open-source, or work projects show real ability relevant to **this** job?

- 9-10: Relevant, non-trivial projects with a clear problem, the candidate's own role, the tools used, and a result (users, accuracy, performance, a live link).
- 7-8: Relevant projects with a clear description and the tools used.
- 5-6: Projects are listed but shallow, generic (tutorial-level), or only partly relevant.
- 3-4: Projects are barely described, or unrelated.
- 1-2: Project names only, with no information.
- Not applicable: no projects section, and the work experience already gives the proof.

For freshers and career changers, projects are often the main evidence, so weigh them seriously.
