## Section: `summary` (Profile / Summary)
Does the opening summary quickly tell a recruiter who this person is and why they fit **this** job?

- 9-10: Short, specific, tailored to the job, with a concrete achievement or a clear specialty.
- 7-8: Clear and relevant, but somewhat generic.
- 5-6: Present but vague ("hardworking team player"), with little job relevance.
- 3-4: Off-target, or just a list of buzzwords.
- 1-2: Confusing, or contradicts the rest of the resume.
- Not applicable: no summary section. Many good resumes skip it.
